The problem

Milk moves early, and it moves on credit.

A distributor sends out loaded vehicles before dawn, and by the end of the month owes an answer to every retailer about what was delivered, what was paid, and what is still outstanding. Registers and spreadsheets keep that answer one person deep — and one argument away from a lost customer. KshirSetu keeps the record straight on both sides of it.

For the office

A web system on the computer

Everything the counter and the accountant do in a day: demand entry, loading sheets, deposit approvals, rates, ledgers, invoices and staff accounts — with role-based permissions so each person sees only their own work.

For your retailers

An Android app for the shop

The retailer places tomorrow's order before the cut-off, records a deposit with its reference and photo, and sees an up-to-date account statement — in Hindi or English, on the phone already in their pocket.

What it covers

The whole day, not one part of it.

Around sixty screens, each one built against how the work is actually done — because it was written alongside a distributor who was already doing it.

01 Daily demand

Retailers order before a cut-off you set per zone. The office sees the day's total by product and by delivery trip, and can enter or correct any retailer's order itself.

02 Loading & distribution

Trip-wise split of the day's load, what each vehicle carries against the capacity it actually has, and printable loading and collection sheets for the driver.

03 Deposits

The retailer records a payment with its UTR and receipt photo; the office approves it against the bank statement — including a checker that reads the statement file and matches references for you.

04 Retailer accounts

A running daily ledger per retailer, opening to closing, with balances, outstanding dues, agreed repayment plans and a watchlist for the ones sliding the wrong way.

05 GST & invoicing

Tax invoices with per-zone financial-year numbering, HSN codes, a GST summary report, and a Tally-compatible export of the sale, bank and cash books.

06 Statements that reach people

Account statements go out as a PDF over WhatsApp or email, one retailer or the whole zone at once — so the monthly reconciliation call stops being a phone call.

07 Crates, damage & stock

Crates issued and returned per retailer, damage and shortage entries, and stock and purchase reports against what the dairy actually supplied.

08 Vehicles & staff

Diesel entries against odometer readings with per-vehicle mileage, plus staff attendance, wage rates and salary payouts for drivers and helpers.

09 Control & oversight

Day-close and freeze so a settled day cannot be edited quietly, an audit trail on rates and approvals, comparison and performance reports, and push reminders when a retailer has not ordered.

How you get it

Your own copy — your data stays yours.

KshirSetu is not a consumer app and it is not a shared pool of everyone's data. Each distributor runs a separate instance with its own database and its own web address. Nothing is shared between distributors.

  1. We talk first. Zones, products, rate structure, how your trips and cut-offs work today, and what your existing data looks like.
  2. Your instance is set up with your firm's name, GSTIN and zones — and your existing retailers and balances are imported and reconciled against your current books.
  3. Your staff get logins with roles, so demand entry, approvals and rate changes stay in the right hands.
  4. Your retailers install the app and enter the dairy code you give them, then sign in with the retailer code and password you issue. It points them at your instance, and only yours.
