KshirSetu

Management software for milk distributors and the retailers they supply.

Milk moves early and on credit. A distributor sends out loaded vehicles before dawn, and by the end of the month owes an answer to every retailer about what was delivered, what was paid, and what is still outstanding. KshirSetu keeps that record straight for both sides.

Who it is for

Milk distributors

A web system for the office — daily demand, trip-wise loading, deposits, retailer ledgers, GST invoices and staff accounts.

Retailers

An Android app for the shop — place tomorrow’s order, record a deposit, and see an up-to-date account statement in Hindi or English.

What the system covers

  • Daily demand — retailers place orders before a cut-off, and the office sees the day’s total by product and by delivery trip.
  • Loading and distribution — what each vehicle carries on each trip, against the capacity it actually has.
  • Deposits — a retailer records a payment with its reference, and the office approves it against the bank statement.
  • Account statements — a running daily ledger per retailer, sent as a PDF by email or WhatsApp.
  • GST invoices — per-zone invoice numbering and Tally-compatible export.
  • Notifications — order reminders and account messages to the retailer’s phone.

How to get it

KshirSetu is not a consumer app. It is supplied to milk distributors, who create an account for each retailer they already do business with. A retailer opens the app, enters the dairy code given by their distributor, and signs in with the retailer code and password issued to them.
